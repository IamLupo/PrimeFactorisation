==============================================================================
12. FINAL INTERPRETATION
==============================================================================

The direct factor-search route is:

    n
     |
     v
    Phi_k(r)
     |
     v
    gcd(n,Phi_k(r))
     |
     v
    hidden factor

The key distinction is now explicit:

1. Direct root existence modulo p/q is easy to characterize.
2. Finding a SMALL integer representative r of that root
   is the difficult search problem.

The experiment therefore measures:
    first successful r
    factor-size scaling
    adaptive-vs-blind performance
    usefulness of higher Phi_k
    usefulness of products
