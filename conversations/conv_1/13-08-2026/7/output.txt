1. EXACT CUBIC-ROOT STRUCTURE
==============================================================================

Phi_3(x) = x^2 + x + 1
Phi_6(x) = x^2 - x + 1

Exact shift:
Phi_6(x) - Phi_3(x-1) = 0
Phi_3(x) - Phi_6(x+1) = 0

Therefore only Phi_3 needs to be studied.

Root condition:
Phi_3(r)=0 mod p  <=>  r^3=1 mod p and r != 1

For prime p != 3, such roots exist exactly when p == 1 mod 3.
