==============================================================================

A3 =
p**2*q**2 + p**2*q + p**2 + p*q**2 + p*q + p + q**2 + q + 1

A6 =
p**2*q**2 - p**2*q + p**2 - p*q**2 + p*q - p + q**2 - q + 1

Dphi = A3 - A6 =
2*(p + q)*(p*q + 1)

Expected:
2*(p+q)*(p*q+1)

Residual:
0

Elementary-symmetric form:
2*e1*e2 + 2*e1

With e1=u and e2=n:
Dphi = 2(n+1)u
