12. COMPUTATIONAL FORM COMPARISON
==============================================================================

Cyclotomic test:
    x = a^E mod n
    Phi_ell(x) mod n
    gcd(n, Phi_ell(x))

Ordinary order test:
    y = a^(ell E) mod n
    gcd(n, y-1)

Both require modular exponentiation.
The cyclotomic test does not eliminate the need for
order-related exponentiation; it only post-processes a^E.

The following experiment therefore focuses on INFORMATION,
not merely arithmetic operation count.
