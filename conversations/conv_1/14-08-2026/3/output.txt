13. INTERPRETATION
==============================================================================
The experiment distinguishes three levels of structure:

LEVEL 1
    F(r) = r^2 + r + 1

LEVEL 2
    F(r) | r^3 - 1
    so r is a cube root of unity modulo F(r).

LEVEL 3
    prime factors ell | F(r)
    should inherit order-3 behaviour when ell is non-exceptional.

The next mathematical question is therefore:

    Can the observed KAPPA residue-filter behaviour
    be rewritten in the algebra of third roots of unity?

A positive result would be substantially stronger than
simply saying that the moduli happen to be F(r).

It would give an algebraic explanation for:
    * the residue classes
    * the dependence between moduli
    * the repeated candidate-density multiplier
    * the exceptional CRT growth
