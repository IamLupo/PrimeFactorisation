5. KEY QUESTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The experiment distinguishes:
    n + exact K + B  -> A
from:
    n alone          -> A ?

The first implication is algebraically certain.
The second is the actual research question.

If multiple auxiliary sequences only reproduce the same A residues, then they are multiple encodings of the same hidden quantity rather than independent sources of information.
