AUXILIARY EXTENSION / K-CANCELLATION TEST

    K' - K = s(1-s)(1-K)

==============================================================================
FINAL REPORT
==============================================================================

N-ONLY ROUTE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    n
     |
     +--> F(r) structure
     |
     +--> simple gcd/congruence tests
     |
     +--> polynomial A(u)
     |
     +--> possible u residues

The experiment intentionally does not insert the hidden A residue.

ORACLE ROUTE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    n + A
       |
       v
    quadratic in u
       |
       v
    discriminant
       |
       v
    p,q

The oracle route succeeds, but is not equivalent to an n-only factoring algorithm.

KEY OBSERVATION TO WATCH
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A genuinely interesting result would be a relation involving only n, auxiliary r-values and u that removes the unknown A/K quantity.

In particular, look for repeated non-random congruences in the N-ONLY AUXILIARY ANNIHILATION section.

If no such relation appears, the next experiment should attack the algebra of multiple auxiliary extensions directly rather than accumulating more CRT residues.
