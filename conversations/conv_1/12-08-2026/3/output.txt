==============================================================================
FINAL INTERPRETATION
==============================================================================

1. EXACT ORACLE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Exact K_r plus known r gives A immediately:

    A = (1-K_r)/F(r)

2. A -> u
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Once A is known, the discriminant condition forces u=p+q.

3. MODULAR EXPERIMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The useful quantity is not merely the number of bits known about A.

Instead we count how many u residues survive:

    A mod M
       ->
    u^2 -(n+1)u+n^2-n+1 = A mod M

4. CRITICAL LIMITATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If the K_r values cannot themselves be computed efficiently from n, then their information cannot be counted as an n-only factoring method.

5. NEXT TARGET
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The strongest future experiment is therefore:

    n
     -> auxiliary structure
     -> constraints on A
     -> surviving u
     -> factor pair

without supplying p, q, A, or K as an oracle.
