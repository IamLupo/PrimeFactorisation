5. STRUCTURAL INTERPRETATION
==============================================================================

This experiment deliberately separates two levels of analysis.

Level 1:

    Given an exact source pair (q1,q3) generated by an n=pq instance,
    the 7-adic projective mechanism can be tested independently.

Level 2:

    A genuine n=pq theorem requires deriving q1(p,q), q3(p,q), H(p,q),
    B(p,q), C(p,q), and the retained matrix from the underlying
    construction.

Therefore this script does not fabricate a symbolic p,q formula.

Once multiple exact source rows from genuinely different n=pq cases are
inserted into CASES, this becomes a controlled family experiment:

    (p,q)
       ->
    (q1,q3)
       ->
    rho
       ->
    k1 + Hensel digits
       ->
    principal 7-adic coordinate.

A future family result is meaningful only if the source pairs themselves
come from the actual n=pq construction.
