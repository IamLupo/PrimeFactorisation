4. SYMMETRIC ALGEBRA
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Exact symmetric identities:
  S = d1 + d2 = -8*n - 1
  P = d1*d2 = 16*n**2 + 4*n + 1
  S^2 - 4P = -3
  Therefore:
    (d1-d2)^2 = S^2 - 4P = -3

Important structural check:
  every symmetric polynomial in d1,d2 reduces to a polynomial
  in S and P, hence to an N-only polynomial.
