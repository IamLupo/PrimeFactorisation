9. DIRECT COMPARISON
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The exact identity says:
    sigma1(N) = N + s + 1
Therefore any successful exact sigma1 reconstruction immediately gives:
    s = sigma1(N) - N - 1
and then:
    p,q = roots of x^2 - s*x + N.

The key distinction is:
    sigma1 accessibility
versus
    H6/H8 resolvent accessibility.
