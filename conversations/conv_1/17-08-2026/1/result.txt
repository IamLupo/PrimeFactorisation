3. PAPER-DRIVEN RESOLVENT IDENTITY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
E1(n) = (n^2 - n + 1) sigma_1(n) - sigma_3(n)
For n=pq:
E1(n) = (p^2-1)(q^2-1)(p+q)
      = s * ((n+1)^2 - s^2)
Hence:
s^3 - (n+1)^2 s + E1(n) = 0
