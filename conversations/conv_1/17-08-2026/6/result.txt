13. FINAL DIAGNOSTIC
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The decisive test is NOT raw mutual information.
It is whether a SMALL REUSABLE N-only character state predicts
a comparable E1-derived character on unseen targets and unseen
moduli, after controlling for state occupancy.

A positive result requires:
  1. meaningful state reuse
  2. OOS accuracy above the matched baseline
  3. improvement for cyclotomic over controls
  4. survival under leave-one-modulus-out
  5. survival under 2D target+modulus holdout
  6. permutation p-values that remain small

A failure would be informative: it would strongly suggest that the
E1 residue correlation seen previously is primarily a property of raw
residue collisions rather than a reusable cyclotomic law.
