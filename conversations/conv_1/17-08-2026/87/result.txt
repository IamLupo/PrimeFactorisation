==============================================================================
3. GENERAL COEFFICIENT SHAPE
==============================================================================
For the first kernel term,
  p^k(1+q)^ell
the coefficient of u^j is
  binom(ell, k-j) * t^(2k-j),
whenever 0 <= k-j <= ell.

For the symmetric term
  q^k(1+p)^ell,
the coefficient is
  binom(ell, k+j) * t^(2k+j),
whenever 0 <= k+j <= ell.

The ell-outer terms contribute the corresponding
negative binomial corrections.
