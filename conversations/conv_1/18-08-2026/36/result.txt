The tested universal boundary pattern is:

Delta_(r,0)(k,d) = binomial(k+r-1, r)

For 1 <= j <= r:
Delta_(r,j)(k,d) =
  (-1)^j / j!
  * C(k+r-1, r-j)
  * product_{m=1}^{j-1} (d-r-m)
  * (d - (r-j)k/(k+j))

The structural consequences are:
  * degree in d = j
  * leading coefficient =
      (-1)^j/j! * C(k+r-1,r-j)

  * fixed roots:
      d = r+1, r+2, ..., r+j-1

  * moving root:
      d = (r-j)k/(k+j)
