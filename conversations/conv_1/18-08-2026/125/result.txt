  p/d operator exists at the tested low complexity.

  inhomogeneous extension:

      q_{p+2}(r)

        sum_a F_a(p,d) q_p(r+a)

      d = D(p)-r.

      INTERIOR:
          reusable propagation operator F_a;

  signal than an unrestricted interpolation.
