9. STRUCTURAL INTERPRETATION
==============================================================================

The purpose of this experiment is to test whether the first 2-adic
obstruction is naturally indexed by the boundary-distance coordinate

    d = D(p) - r.

There are three possible outcomes:

    A. same d gives the same defect row;
       then d alone may explain the repeated directions;

    B. same d splits into several defect rows;
       then p or r carries independent information;

    C. d-group ranks remain large;
       then the defect is not naturally boundary-distance indexed.

The test deliberately uses exact row equality and exact F_2 ranks.

No interpolation is involved.

In particular, a repeated row vector across different p values is
reported as an observed equality, not promoted to a general formula.

The central question is therefore whether the rank-3 obstruction can
already be organized by the finite boundary coordinate d.
