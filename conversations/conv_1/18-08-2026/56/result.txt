    Q_j(K,D) = sum_m a_{j,m}(K) (D-D0(j))^m

The key question is whether the higher coefficients are structurally generated from a_{j,0}(K).

A low-degree exact ratio
    a_{j,m}(K) / a_{j,0}(K)
would be meaningful.

A generic degree-5 / degree-5 rational expression obtained from six interpolation points is NOT treated as evidence.

The same restriction applies to successive ratios
    a_{j,m+1}(K) / a_{j,m}(K).

The clean boundary ladders remain:
  j=0 : (K+1)(K+2)(K+3)(K+4)
  j=1 : (K+2)(K+3)(K+4)
  j=2 : (K+3)(K+4)
  j=3 : (K+4)
  j=4 : 1
  j=5 : (K-5)(K-7)(K-9)(K-11)

Fresh K values are needed to distinguish genuine rational structure from finite interpolation.
