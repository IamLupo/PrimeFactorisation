10. STRUCTURAL INTERPRETATION
==============================================================================

Experiment 116 ruled out obvious rank collapse after several
natural coefficient normalizations.

The degree-3 A/B nullspaces in Experiment 116 were not treated
as discoveries because the systems were underdetermined.

Experiment 117 therefore asks a different question:

    Does the residual row q[p,r] propagate in p by a fixed
    finite-band operator in the falling index r?

A positive result would give an actual construction mechanism:

    q[p+2,r] = sum_a f_a(r) q[p,r+a].

The universal operator test is substantially stronger than
fitting each transition independently.

The p/r operator test then checks whether a small indexed
correction is enough when a purely r-dependent operator fails.

Only unique exact solutions are highlighted as structural.
Underdetermined systems are reported but not promoted.

Everything is exact over QQ.
No floating point.
No SymPy.
No extrapolation.
