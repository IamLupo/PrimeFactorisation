EXPERIMENT: EXACT INVERSE OF THE MONOTONE X-LAYER ORACLE
==============================================================================================================

OBJECTIVE
  Given N and the exact reference value R_TRUE, recover X_TRUE
  by monotone search only.

  During the search:
    - p is not used
    - q is not used
    - p+q is not used
    - the discriminant is not used
    - only N, R_TRUE, and monotonicity are used

==============================================================================================================
INTERPRETATION
==============================================================================================================

  The experiment establishes the inverse side of the oracle:

      (N, R_TRUE) -> unique positive X

  by monotone binary search.

  The search never tests candidate primes.
  The discriminant is not used during the search.

  IMPORTANT:
    R_TRUE is still supplied from the hidden X.
    Therefore this does NOT yet provide a factoring algorithm.

  The remaining research question is:

      Can R_TRUE be generated from N
      without first knowing X or p,q?
