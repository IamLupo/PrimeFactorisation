INTERPRETATION

  Experiment 461 proved that, inside one instance,
  every deterministic function of (N,S,d,r) is fixed
  across the j-fibre.

  Experiment 462 asks a different question:
  whether the hidden j values across the eight test
  instances obey a simple common law in the observed data.

  A relation is interesting only if it is:
    1. exact,
    2. low-complexity,
    3. small-coefficient, and
    4. survives leave-one-out validation.

  Arbitrary interpolation is explicitly excluded.

LIMITATION

  Failure to find a relation in these bounded families
  does NOT prove that no more complicated cross-instance
  relation exists.

NEXT INTERPRETATION

  If no stable low-complexity law exists, the eight
  observed tuples provide no evidence that j can be
  inferred from a simple shared formula.

  If a small exact law survives leave-one-out, it becomes
  a concrete candidate for an independent-data audit.
