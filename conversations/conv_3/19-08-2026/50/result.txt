INTERPRETATION
========================================================================================================================
  Experiments 424-426 measured modular filtering primarily as candidate-by-candidate arithmetic.
  Experiment 427 changes only the representation: every prime becomes an exact survivor bitset.
  Once masks are built, candidate filtering is performed through exact integer bitwise intersection.
  This separates two costs:
    1. constructing the modular masks
    2. applying fixed or adaptive intersections
  The adaptive selector can now inspect all unused primes using bitset intersections rather than recomputing every modular condition for every surviving candidate.
  The experiment therefore tests whether the poor work ratio observed in Experiment 425 was mainly caused by the candidate-wise evaluation model.
  A surviving modular bit is still only a necessary condition.
  Exact integer gap-square reconstruction remains the final acceptance condition.
  Nothing in this experiment establishes a factoring theorem.
