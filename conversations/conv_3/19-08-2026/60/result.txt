QUESTION

  contained in the exact f relation?

CORE
  f(x,K) = x^2 + 2*d0*x + 4*K-r

IDENTITY

MEASUREMENTS
  admissible x residues modulo 2^u
  distinct induced K residues modulo 2^(u-2)
  average number of x residues per K residue

  Factor-gap reconstruction is checked independently.
  It is NOT used to manufacture or prune K residues.
