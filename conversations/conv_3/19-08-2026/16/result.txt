EXACT X-BRANCH / DISCRIMINANT RECONSTRUCTION

Objective:
  1. Correct the discriminant relation from Experiment 392.
  2. Verify:
       Delta_X = (N+3-2*X_TRUE)^2
  3. Verify the equivalent factor form:
       sqrt(Delta_X) = (p-1)(q-1) - (p+q)
  4. Recover both X roots directly from Delta_X.
  5. Determine whether the smaller X branch is always X_TRUE.
  6. Express X_TRUE using N, K and sqrt(Delta_X).
  7. Determine whether any sign ambiguity remains.

==============================================================================================================
EXPERIMENT 393 FINAL STATUS
==============================================================================================================

The corrected discriminant identity is:

  Delta_X
    = (N+3)^2 - 4(K+N^2+2)
    = (X_OTHER-X_TRUE)^2

For X_TRUE = p+q+1:

  X_OTHER-X_TRUE
    = N-2(p+q)+1
    = (p-1)(q-1) - (p+q)

Thus the exact branches are:

  X_TRUE  = (N+3-sqrt(Delta_X))/2
  X_OTHER = (N+3+sqrt(Delta_X))/2

The important remaining question is not whether the
quadratic has two exact branches — that is established.

The important question is whether the smaller branch
can be identified from N alone, or from a quantity
that is independently computable from N, without
already possessing K or the hidden factor information.
