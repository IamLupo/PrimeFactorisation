EXACT X-RECONSTRUCTION / K-ELIMINATION AUDIT

Goal:
  1. Treat X as the primary hidden variable.
  2. Use:
       K = -N^2 + N*X - X^2 + 3X - 2
  3. Verify this identity exactly across all instances.
  4. Substitute K back into Q_X and verify complete collapse.
  5. Determine what information about X remains encoded in K.
  6. Test whether K + N^2 + 2 factorizes naturally in terms of
       X, N, S=p+q.
  7. Test whether simple transforms of K recover S or X.
  8. Search for low-complexity identities involving only N and K.

==============================================================================================================
EXPERIMENT 394 FINAL STATUS
==============================================================================================================

The key identity being audited is:

  K = -N^2 + N*X - X^2 + 3X - 2

Using X = S+1 gives:

  K = -N^2 + N*S - S^2 + 2S

and therefore:

  S^2 - (N+2)S + (K+N^2) = 0

This is the same quadratic structure one level lower:

  X = S+1
  S = p+q

The research question is now sharper:

  Can the smaller root S=p+q, or X=S+1,
  be reconstructed from N alone through a
  structurally simpler invariant?

In particular, any unexpectedly simple expression
for Delta_S or K+N^2 is worth investigating.
