==============================================================================================================
EXPERIMENT 400 START
==============================================================================================================

EXACT DIRECT N,d -> FACTOR-PAIR RECONSTRUCTION

Objective:
  1. Start only from N and K.
  2. Recover:
       D = -4K - 3N^2 + 6N + 1
       d = sqrt(D)
  3. Eliminate S and g as explicit reconstruction stages.
  4. Form the direct radicand:
       G = ((N+1-d)^2 - 16N) / 4
     so that:
       G = (q-p)^2
  5. Recover the factor gap directly from N,d:
       g = sqrt(G)
  6. Recover p,q directly from N,d,g.
  7. Verify the equivalent one-line expressions:
       p = (N-d-7-2g)/4 + 2
       q = (N-d-7+2g)/4 + 2
  8. Test whether the complete chain can be represented by
     nested exact square roots with no S-variable.
  9. Search for additional simplifications of the direct
     factor radicand G.
 10. Audit whether G has a simpler factor form involving d
     and low-degree expressions in N.

No resultants are constructed.
No symbolic multivariate factoring is performed.
Only exact integer arithmetic is used.
