  K = A/4 - z^2

square-residue set modulo 2^b.

No (K,x) witness tree is stored.

INTERPRETATION

  The previous witness-tree experiment failed because
  the number of x witnesses grows exponentially.

  The completed-square transformation removes that cost:

      K = A/4 - z^2

  Therefore the K image is exactly an affine copy of
  the quadratic-residue set modulo 2^b.

  Its cardinality can be computed without enumerating
  the x domain and without constructing the K image.

  The image-density behavior is therefore an intrinsic
  property of the 2-adic square map, rather than an
  artifact of a particular implementation.

  This experiment does NOT show that K can be recovered
  as an unrestricted integer.

  It isolates exactly how much information the 2-adic
  equation contributes to K.
