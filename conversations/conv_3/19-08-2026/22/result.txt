EXACT SINGLE-IDENTITY FACTORIZATION OF THE N,d,g CHAIN

Objective:
  1. Recover d from:
       D = -4K - 3N^2 + 6N + 1 = d^2
  2. Recover S from:
       S = (N+1-d)/2
  3. Recover the factor gap:
       g = sqrt(S^2 - 4N)
  4. Test the compressed identity:
       (N-d-7)^2 - 4g^2 = 16(d+3)
  5. Factor it exactly as:
       (N-d-7-2g)(N-d-7+2g) = 16(d+3)
  6. Divide the two factors by 4 and test:
       (N-d-7-2g)/4 = p-2
       (N-d-7+2g)/4 = q-2
  7. Determine whether the entire factor pair is encoded
     directly by the single difference-of-squares identity.
  8. Test equivalent factorizations involving only N,d,g.
  9. Measure whether the new factorized form is structurally
     simpler than the two successive quadratic reconstructions.

No resultants are constructed.
No symbolic multivariate factoring is performed.
Only exact integer arithmetic is used.
