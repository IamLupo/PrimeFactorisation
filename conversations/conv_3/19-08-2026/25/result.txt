==============================================================================================================
EXPERIMENT 402 START
==============================================================================================================

EXACT ELIMINATION OF d / DIRECT N,K -> FACTOR POLYNOMIAL

Objective:
  1. Start from N and K only.
  2. Use the previously established relation:
       2Z^2 - (N+1-d)Z + 2N = 0
  3. Solve this equation formally for d:
       d = [2Z^2 - (N+1)Z + 2N] / Z
  4. Eliminate d using:
       d^2 = -4K - 3N^2 + 6N + 1
  5. Clear denominators exactly.
  6. Obtain a polynomial P(N,K,Z) with no d.
  7. Verify that Z=p and Z=q are exact roots.
  8. Determine the degree and coefficient structure.
  9. Test whether the quartic has additional exact roots.
 10. Test whether the quartic factors using N alone, K alone,
     or the known factor polynomial Z^2-(p+q)Z+N.
 11. Compare the eliminated polynomial with the direct
     quadratic from Experiment 401.

No resultant is constructed.
No symbolic multivariate factoring is performed.
Only exact integer arithmetic is used.
