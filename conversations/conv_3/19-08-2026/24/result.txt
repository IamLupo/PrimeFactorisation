EXACT RADICAL CHAIN CORRECTION / DIRECT FACTOR POLYNOMIAL AUDIT

Objective:
  1. Correct the factor-sum audit from Experiment 400.
  2. Remove non-structural divisibility tests from pass/fail.
  3. Verify the direct N,d -> g -> p,q reconstruction.
  4. Verify:
       p+q = (N+1-d)/2
       pq  = N
  5. Eliminate g completely and verify that p and q are
     the two roots of a quadratic whose coefficients depend
     only on N and d.
  6. Construct the direct factor quadratic:
       Z^2 - ((N+1-d)/2) Z + N = 0
  7. Verify its exact discriminant:
       Delta_Z = ((N+1-d)/2)^2 - 4N = g^2
  8. Compare this quadratic directly with the original
     shifted-factor representation.
  9. Search for a denominator-free equivalent quadratic
     involving only N,d,Z.
 10. Determine whether the entire factorization layer
     reduces to a single quadratic after the first radical.

No resultants are constructed.
No symbolic multivariate factoring is performed.
Only exact integer arithmetic is used.
