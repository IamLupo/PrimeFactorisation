EXACT COMPRESSED INVARIANT / LOW-COMPLEXITY FACTORIZATION AUDIT

Objective:
  1. Compute the compressed invariant:
       D = -4K - 3N^2 + 6N + 1
  2. Verify exactly that D = d^2.
  3. Test whether D admits additional low-complexity forms.
  4. Test shifted forms involving N and d.
  5. Test gcd relationships with N, N+1, N+2, N+3.
  6. Verify the factor identity:
       d+3 = (p-2)(q-2)
  7. Search for simple relations between d and N.
  8. Verify reconstruction of S and X using only N and d.
  9. Record exact size and divisibility signatures.

No resultants are constructed.
No symbolic factoring of large multivariate expressions is performed.
