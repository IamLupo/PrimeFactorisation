EXPERIMENT 395 START
==============================================================================================================

EXACT S-QUADRATIC / CORRECTED K-ELIMINATION

Purpose:
  1. Correct the S = X-1 substitution from Experiment 394.
  2. Verify:
       K = -N^2 + N*S - S^2 + S + N
  3. Verify the exact S quadratic:
       S^2 - (N+1)S + (K+N^2-N) = 0
  4. Recover the second S root.
  5. Verify:
       K + N^2 - N = S*(N-S+1)
  6. Verify the factor form:
       S*(N-S+1) = (p+q)(p-1)(q-1)
  7. Verify the corrected S discriminant.
  8. Verify that the S and X discriminants are identical.
  9. Determine whether S=p+q is always the smaller branch.
