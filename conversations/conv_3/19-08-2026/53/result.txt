INTERPRETATION
========================================================================================================================
  Experiment 430 moves several necessary quadratic-residue conditions before exact f evaluation.
  The wheel consists of residue classes modulo the product of the selected small primes.
  A d outside the wheel cannot satisfy the exact gap-square condition.
  Therefore such d values can be rejected before computing the exact k/f relation.
  The experiment measures candidate-generation work, not the mathematical strength of factorization.
  Exact gap-square reconstruction remains authoritative.
  This is an implementation optimization experiment, not a factorization theorem.
