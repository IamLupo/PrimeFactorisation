INTERPRETATION
========================================================================================================================
  This experiment measures the REAL f-candidate population.
  The wheel is applied to d before the f predicate.

  Unlike Experiment 434, no candidate test is defined relative
  to the true d. The f predicate is derived from the original
  exact K congruence.

  The important scaling quantities are:
    wheel density = wheel hits / interval
    f density    = genuine f-candidates / interval
    prefilter    = wheel hits / genuine f-candidates

  Exact factor reconstruction remains authoritative.
  This remains a candidate-isolation experiment, not a
  factorization theorem.
