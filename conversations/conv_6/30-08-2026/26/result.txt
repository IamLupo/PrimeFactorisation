    a*b ≡ g (mod r1)

and therefore:

    b ≡ g*a^(-1) (mod r1)

Write:

    p = a + k*r1
    q = b + l*r1

Because r2 is close to r1, let:

    delta = r2-r1

    p mod r2 = a-k*delta (mod r2)
    q mod r2 = b-l*delta (mod r2)

The product condition becomes:

    (a-k*delta)(b-l*delta) ≡ g (mod r2)

For fixed a and k this is LINEAR in l:

    (-x*delta) l ≡ g-x*b (mod r2)

where:

    x = a-k*delta

    (a,k) states
