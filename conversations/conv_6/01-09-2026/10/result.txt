ALGEBRAIC IDENTITIES

    p = r1*k + a
    q = r2*l + b

    n = (r1*k+a)(r2*l+b)

    n = R*K + r1*k*b + r2*l*a + a*b

    c1 = k*b/r2
    c2 = l*a/r1
    c3 = a*b/R

    c1+c2+c3
        = (n-R*K)/R

    E = floor(c1+c2+c3)

The experiment therefore checks whether the C1/C2/C3
representation gives useful pruning beyond the known
