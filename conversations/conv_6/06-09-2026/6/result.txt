START EXPERIMENT 164
========================================================================
CRT Interval Lift After Multiplicative C-Propagation

Experiment 163 showed that C-product propagation
does not determine the residue vectors by itself.

But A=[p mod r_i] and B=[q mod s_j] are not
independent variables: each complete vector must
be the CRT representation of one actual integer.

Since p < sqrt(n), a CRT modulus M > sqrt(n)
makes a residue vector correspond to at most one p.

This experiment searches only the cheapest subset
of residue variables whose modulus product exceeds
sqrt(n), then checks the resulting integer against
all remaining residue domains and n = p*q.
