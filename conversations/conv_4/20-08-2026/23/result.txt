MAIN RESULT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  M_x = (p+x)(q+x)
      = x^2 + S*x + N.

Its discriminant as a polynomial in x is

  Delta = S^2 - 4N = (p-q)^2.

  M(+x)M(-x)
    = (N+x^2)^2 - S^2*x^2.

  S^2
    = ((N+x^2)^2-M(+x)M(-x))/x^2.

  Delta

  Delta = (N+1)^2 - M(+1)M(-1).
