[15] FORMAL THEOREM
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Let
  F_n = p(q+1)^n + q(p+1)^n
        - (q+1)p^n - (p+1)q^n.

Define
  H_s = det(F_(s+i+j))_(0<=i,j<4).

Then
  H_s = [N(N+S+1)]^(s+1)
        * (4N-S^2)^2
        * (4N-S^2+1)^2.

Therefore, whenever H_s != 0:
  H_(s+1)/H_s = N(N+S+1)
  M1 = H_(s+1)/(N H_s)
  S = M1-N-1

and finally
  z^2-Sz+N=0
recovers the factor pair.
