[16] FORMAL THEOREM
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Let
  F_n = p(q+1)^n + q(p+1)^n
        - (q+1)p^n - (p+1)q^n.

Write
  H_s = det(F_{s+i+j})_(0<=i,j<4).

Then F_n has four exponential bases
  lambda = {p,q,p+1,q+1},
with weights
  c = {-(q+1),-(p+1),p,q}.

Hence
  H_s
  = [product(c_i)]
    [product(lambda_i)]^s
    [Vandermonde(lambda)]^2.

For this kernel:
  product(c_i)
  = product(lambda_i)
  = pq(p+1)(q+1)
  = N(N+S+1).

Furthermore:
  Vandermonde(lambda)^2
  = (p-q)^4 * ((p-q)^2-1)^2
  = (4N-S^2)^2
    * (4N-S^2+1)^2.

Therefore:
  H_s
  = [N(N+S+1)]^(s+1)
    * (4N-S^2)^2
    * (4N-S^2+1)^2.

Whenever H_s != 0:
  M1 = H_(s+1)/(N*H_s)
  S  = M1 - N - 1.

Finally:
  z^2 - S*z + N = 0
recovers p and q.
