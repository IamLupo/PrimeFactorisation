   prime residue classes modulo 4.

      y^2-x^2+3x-2=v
   completes to
      (2y-2x+3)(2y+2x-3)=n      if n==3 mod 4
      (2y-2x+3)(2y+2x-3)=3n    if n==1 mod 4
4. Therefore finding an integer (x,y) solution with the required
   factor sign/positivity constraints is essentially equivalent
   to finding a factor pair of n (or 3n).

   v==g branch, gcd(b-x-y+2,n)=q for the stated x,y definitions.
