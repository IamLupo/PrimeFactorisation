[7] SHIFT CONDITIONS FOR FACTOR EXPOSURE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Modulo p:

  M_x = (p+x)(q+x)
      == x(q+x) (mod p)

Therefore p divides M_x exactly when:

  x == 0 (mod p)
  or
  x == -q (mod p)

Modulo q:

  M_x == x(p+x) (mod q)

Therefore q divides M_x exactly when:

  x == 0 (mod q)
  or
  x == -p (mod q)

The nontrivial conditions require knowledge related to the unknown complementary factor.

[9] TWO-SHIFT MOD-N CHANNEL
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Modulo N:

  M_a == a(S+a)
  M_b == b(S+b)

Thus, when gcd(a-b,N)=1, the difference channel contains S modulo N.

MAIN RESULT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The shifted-product algebra now gives two distinct channels:

  1. Symmetric channel:
       M(+x)+M(-x) = 2(N+x^2)

  2. Antisymmetric channel:
       M(+x)-M(-x) = 2Sx

The opposite-shift product gives S^2:

  S^2 = ((N+x^2)^2-M(+x)M(-x))/x^2

and therefore the actual gap-square is:

  Delta=(p-q)^2
       = ((N+x^2)^2-M(+x)M(-x))/x^2 - 4N

For x=1:
  S^2 = (N+1)^2-M(+1)M(-1)
  Delta = (N+1)^2-M(+1)M(-1)-4N

The GCD search tests whether this shifted-product information leaks a factor directly through modular structure.
