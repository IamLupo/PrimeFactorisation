    v2(a) = v2(A)-1
    v2(b) = v2(B)-1

    future_steps

        v2(A),
        v2(B),

    depth

    2 + future_steps

    1 +
    min(
        v2(A),
        v2(B),
        v2(A-B)-1

If:

    v2(A) != v2(B),

then:

    v2(A-B) = min(v2(A),v2(B)).

    v2(A) = v2(B),

    v2(A-B) > v2(A)

or A-B=0.

    depth

    1 + min(
        v2(A),
