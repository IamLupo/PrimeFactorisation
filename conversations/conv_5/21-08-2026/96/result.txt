==========================================================================================
FINAL STRUCTURAL SUMMARY
==========================================================================================

Exact theorem:

    depth = v2(gcd(2A, 2B, n+c))

Equivalently:

    depth = v2(gcd(2A, n+c))
          = v2(gcd(2B, n+c))

The symmetric gcd therefore has the form:

    G = 2^depth * odd_part(G)

This experiment asks whether the odd part of G
contains any structurally relevant information, or
whether the complete depth mechanism is genuinely
the 2-adic valuation alone.

The factor-free n-only gcd family is also tested.
Because gcd(n+c,n+r)=gcd(n+c,r-c), such a fixed
shift family can only produce a bounded truncation
of v2(n+c).
