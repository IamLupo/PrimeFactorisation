==========================================================================================
FINAL STRUCTURAL SUMMARY
==========================================================================================

Established from the previous experiments:

    M = n+c
    M = 2^w R
    R odd

    H = gcd(C,M)
    odd(H) = gcd(C,R)

Experiment 684 showed:

    odd(C) | R

is false for most states.

Experiment 685 showed:

    R + w does not uniquely determine H

from divisor information alone.

The new experiment tests whether this information
loss can be repaired by recursively transforming
the odd core:

    R -> odd(R+3)
    R -> odd(R+9)

The target questions are:

    1. Does recursive descent reveal odd(C)?
    2. Does recursive descent reveal v2(C)?
    3. Does recursive descent determine depth?
    4. Does recursive descent recover odd(H)?

A positive result would give evidence for a genuine
recursive 'factor n+c, strip 2, retarget' mechanism.

A negative result would establish that the first
shifted-core transformation loses information that
cannot be recovered by the same small-shift recursion.
