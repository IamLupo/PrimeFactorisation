    ACTUAL FACTORIZATION:

        F_k(n)

          some odd factor residues p,q satisfy
          p*q == n (mod 2^k)
          and produce this m
        }.

The candidate exact fiber theorem is:

    F_k(n)
      =
    {1,2,...,T}

where

    T = min(
        v2(n+9),

        v2(n+3),
        k
    )

        m=1,2,3,4,5.

2-adic residual depth, not necessarily the depth of
the actual prime factorization.
