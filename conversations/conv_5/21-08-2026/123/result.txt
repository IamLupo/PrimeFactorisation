==========================================================================================
FINAL STRUCTURAL SUMMARY
==========================================================================================

The exact identity is:

    H = gcd(C,n+c)

Writing:

    n+c = 2^w R
    R odd

we always have:

    odd(H) = gcd(C,R)

Experiment 684 showed that:

    odd(C) | R
is only true for a minority of states.

Therefore the useful object supplied by factoring R is
not generally the whole canonical residual C.

The new question is:

    Can R + w determine H?

and then:

    Can H + frame determine C?

and finally:

    Can C determine the factor pair?

This separates the proposed 'factor n+c repeatedly'
idea into three precise information channels.
