Let:

    d = gcd(A,B)

and for A,B != 0:

    A=d*a
    B=d*b
    gcd(a,b)=1.

Therefore the normalized pair has exactly three
possible parity classes:

    (a,b)=(odd,odd)
    (a,b)=(odd,even)
    (a,b)=(even,odd).

No (even,even) state is possible.

FRAME A:

    X = d(b-a)/2
    Y = d(a+b)/2.

Hence:

    gcd(X,Y)

    d
      when a,b are both odd,

      when a,b have opposite parity.

    3 | (3a-b)

    gcd(X,Y)

    d
      when a,b are both odd,

      when a,b have opposite parity.

    a,b both odd

    depth =
        v2(gcd(A,B))
