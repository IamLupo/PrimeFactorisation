  F_k(N) = (N mod 2^k, floor(sqrt(N)) mod 2^k)

INTERPRETATION
--------------
1. The key quantity is max_k as N grows.
2. If max_k remains roughly constant, that supports
   a bounded 2-adic fingerprint hypothesis.
3. If max_k grows with the bit length of N, the earlier
   2^10 result was probably a scale-dependent effect.
4. Also compare mean_k and k/log2(N).
5. The fixed-k coverage table shows whether a practical
   constant number of low bits survives scaling.
