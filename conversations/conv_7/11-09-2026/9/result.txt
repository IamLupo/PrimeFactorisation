2-ADIC BRANCH-FINGERPRINT EXPERIMENT
------------------------------------

For each semiprime:
  N = p*q
  s = floor(sqrt(N))
  D = N-s^2
  4*x2+3*s = t*q

Question:
  Does increasing 2-adic information from
  (N,s,D) make t increasingly deterministic?

KEY OBSERVATION TO WATCH
------------------------
If larger powers of two substantially increase
the deterministic-group percentage, that suggests
the hidden branch is leaving a 2-adic fingerprint.

If the percentage quickly plateaus, then the missing
q mod 4 information is probably not recoverable from
N,s,D through simple 2-adic data alone.

INTERPRETATION
--------------
1. t is already exactly characterized if q is known.
2. This experiment tests whether N and sqrt(N)-derived
   residues leak enough information to identify t.
3. Pay attention to the transition M=4 -> 8 -> 16 -> ...
4. Pay attention to whether q mod 4 becomes deterministic.
