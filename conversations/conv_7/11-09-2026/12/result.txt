INTERPRETATION
--------------
1. This dataset deliberately has s > 4096.
2. Therefore s mod 4096 is no longer the exact s.
3. A k=12 collision here is much stronger evidence
   against a fixed 12-bit fingerprint.
4. If k=12 remains collision-free, that is substantially
   more interesting than the previous exhaustive result.
5. Examine same-s versus different-s collisions and
   the delta_s/delta_D structure.
